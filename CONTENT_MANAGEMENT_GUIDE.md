# Updating the ICBS-LAB website

The website editor works in your browser. You do not need to install anything.
The person who invites you will provide the correct laboratory editor bookmark.

## Sign in and choose the review branch

1. Open the bookmark supplied by your laboratory maintainer, or [Pages CMS](https://app.pagescms.org/).
2. Use the sign-in option for your invited email address and follow the email prompt.
3. Open **research-group-page-demo** and choose **cms-drafts**. Ask your inviter for help if either is missing.
4. Keep using this bookmark. Do not switch to **main**.

**Save sends work for review; it does not publish it.** Tell your reviewer which
entries you changed when you are ready. The reviewer checks and publishes them.
Only check the public website after the reviewer confirms publication.

## Members and coauthors

Open **Members and coauthors**. Choose a person to edit, or create a new entry.

- Enter the full name, including accents and Turkish characters. Give the academic role when known.
- For a new entry, choose its **Web address** once, such as `jane-doe`. Use lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens. Leave it unchanged on later visits—even if the person's name changes.
- Choose exactly one category: Professor, Graduate Student, Undergraduate Student, or Alumni.
- To archive someone who has left the lab, change their category to **Alumni**. Keep their name and photograph; this preserves their paper references.
- For a paper author who is not a laboratory member, choose **External coauthor**. They will not appear on Members. Do not invent their role or affiliation.
- Add a biography and choose or upload a photograph. Choose a portrait with some space around the face because the website displays a square crop.
- In **Email and social links**, add a row and choose Email, LinkedIn, Google Scholar, Personal website, GitHub, or Faculty profile. For Email, enter the ordinary email address. Otherwise paste the full web address, starting with `https://`.
- Smaller **Display order** numbers appear first within a category. You can usually leave this alone.

Save, then ask your reviewer to check the result. Deletion is disabled to protect
publication references. Ask your maintainer about duplicate or mistaken entries.

## Projects

Open **Projects**, then choose an existing project or create one.

1. Enter the title and a short summary. Choose the Web address once for a new project.
2. Write the full description using the formatting buttons. The image button inserts additional research images. Give each image a useful description.
3. Choose a featured image for project cards and the detail page.
4. Add external links using their complete web addresses. Select related publications if needed; papers linked from Publications also appear automatically.
5. Turn on **Feature on homepage** if requested by the lab. The homepage has three slots; the three featured projects with the smallest Display order numbers appear.
6. Leave **Draft** on while preparing the project. Turn it off when ready for approval, save, and notify your reviewer.

Turning Draft off still requires review before publication. Turning it on for an
existing project requests removal from public listings after approval; ask the
reviewer first because existing links to that page may stop working.

## Publications

Open **Publications**, then edit or create an entry.

1. Copy the exact paper title and verified publication date. If only the year is known, ask your maintainer; do not invent a day.
2. Select every author **in the published order**. Add a missing person in Members and coauthors first, then return to the paper. Create external coauthors only once.
3. Choose the publication kind and enter the full conference or journal name. Add publisher, pages and abstract when verified.
4. Paste the DOI identifier without `https://doi.org/`. For example, enter `10.1109/ICM66518.2025.11322489`.
5. Under **Publisher and other web links**, add the publisher URL. For a PDF already online, choose **PDF web address** and paste its URL. Alternatively use **PDF document → Upload PDF**. Only upload a version the lab has permission to share.
6. Paste the complete **BibTeX citation** exported by the publisher or Google Scholar. Do not rewrite it from memory. Existing papers keep their existing citation until you enter a replacement here.
7. Select related projects. Add optional notes, check the author order, turn Draft off when ready, save, and notify your reviewer.

DOI lookup and automatic paper import are not part of this editor. Ask a maintainer
if you need help obtaining citation information.

## Homepage, contact information, and images

Use **Homepage wording and photograph** to change the introduction, team photo,
or research wording. Use **Contact information** for verified contact details;
include the official source web address. The site layout is managed separately.

Image pickers accept JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP. Use the best original available;
avoid screenshots of photographs. Upload a replacement, select it, then save the
entry. Uploading alone does not change the page. Do not rename or remove existing
files from the media library: another page may use them. Ask a maintainer to clean
up unused files.

**News is for draft preparation only.** Saved news articles are not published or
linked from the website during this first release.

## Mistakes, errors, and help

- Before saving: correct the fields, or leave the entry without saving.
- After saving but before publication: reopen the entry and correct it; tell your reviewer what happened.
- After publication: contact your reviewer or the person who invited you. They can restore an earlier version.
- If Save fails or a review check fails: keep your text, note the entry name and error, and contact your inviter. Do not change the Web address to try to fix it.
- If you see a login or permission error, ask your inviter to check your approved email address and access.

Never enter passwords, private keys, unpublished personal information, or other
secrets into website fields. Content approved for publication is public.
